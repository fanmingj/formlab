01

Set your camera to the side.

Use a fixed camera, roughly perpendicular to the movement. Diagonal and front views distort the angles used here.

02

Give your movement some room.

Keep the required joints in frame throughout the set. Avoid baggy clothing, obstructions, moving cameras, and other people.

03

Include the start and finish.

Record at least three complete reps if possible, with a brief pause in the starting position. Select up to 90 seconds to analyze.

04

Use the review as a second look.

These are projected angles and pattern-based cues, not a diagnosis or a guarantee of correct form. Review the video yourself and discuss uncertain technique with a trainer.
BUILT AROUND YOUR PRIVACY

Your video stays with you.

The app loads Google’s MediaPipe pose model and runtime from Google and jsDelivr. Those services receive ordinary asset requests, but your video frames are processed locally in a browser worker.

The pose model finds body landmarks. Visible joints are used to estimate 2D angles; explicit movement rules detect reps and generate review cues. There is no remote chat model, upload endpoint, account, or analytics service.

Reports are saved only when you choose Save. Browser storage is not encrypted, and clearing browser data removes your history. Exported reports may contain your video filename and movement measurements.

Works best with a recent Chrome or Edge browser. Internet access is needed to load the model unless its assets are already cached.
